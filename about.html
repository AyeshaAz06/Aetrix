At Aetrix, we believe good content does more than explain. It helps people understand complex ideas, make informed decisions, and use technology with ease.

About Aetrix

Aetrix was founded by a group of software documentation enthusiasts and information design experts who have spent more than twenty years working with cybersecurity, identity management, and finance products - the kind of software where a missing sentence in a manual can mean a support ticket or even worse, a compliance gap.

That experience and insights shape how Aetrix works. We don't treat product knowledge, content strategy, documentation, help center management, and publishing as separate disciplines. They're part of the same chain, seen at different distances, answering questions like:

What does this system do, who needs to understand it, and how do we say that clearly and keep it true as the system changes?

We work directly with founders, engineering leads, and marketing teams because the fastest way to write something accurately is to talk to the people who built it.

Principle 01

Documentation as infrastructure

Content is versioned, reviewed, and maintained like code, not written once and left to go outdated.

Principle 02

Written by someone who reads the system

We sit with your engineers, your policies, and your product rather than rely solely on marketing brief or assumptions.

Principle 03

Clarity by design

A well-defined, disciplined process is what holds the entire system together. Without it, timelines slip, quality becomes difficult to maintain at scale, consistency suffers, and visibility blurs. We can implement clear, transparent, and accountable processes to ensure that every draft, review, and update follows a structured path that your team can track and defend with confidence.


The Process

Kick-off meeting
Install the application build and explore it
Interview developers and SMEs
Analyze the information and identify knowledge gaps
Map new content and areas requiring updates
Draft and structure the content
Review internally and with relevant stakeholders
Publish
Monitor customer feedback and update the docs as needed

Research-led content, human verified.

Whether you need sharp social media posts, in-depth blogs, or long-form eBooks, we can transform intricate concepts into simple, engaging reads. We deliver well-researched brand and technical copy covering cybersecurity, compliance, risk management, Microsoft ecosystems, and similar domains.

Content is AI-assisted, yet human verified. AI speeds up research and drafting, but that’s what it’s all about. All facts, claims, and technical details are revised and verified by a person before shipping.
